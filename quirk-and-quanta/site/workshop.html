⚠️ Why Exact SKUs Fail with Dense Vector Retrieval

Dense vector models (like Gemini) use subword tokenizers (Byte-Pair Encoding). An arbitrary SKU code such as QQ-0015 is fragmented into meaningless sub-tokens like ["QQ", "-", "00", "15"].

In 768-dimensional semantic latent space, these fragments project close to general digits, year dates, or generic physics tokens rather than clustering specifically on product QQ-0015. Dense cosine similarity measures semantic topicality rather than exact symbol equality.

The Engineering Fix: Okapi BM25 builds an inverted index over whole tokens. The rare exact token qq-0015 receives an extraordinarily high Inverse Document Frequency (IDF) weight, guaranteeing #1 ranking. Hybrid RRF combines both worlds.
